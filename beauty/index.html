A quick guide

Choosing a lipstick

Finding the right shade

Type a shade name, brand or finish (matte, liquid, nude) in the search bar. The catalogue updates instantly so you can narrow results quickly.

Price & availability

Displayed prices are indicative. Always confirm final pricing, stock status and shipping terms on the product page before purchasing.

Matte vs liquid

Matte finishes offer a soft, non-shiny look that lasts longer. Liquid formulas deliver intense pigment and a modern, high-impact finish.

Everyday vs statement

Nudes and soft pinks work beautifully for daily wear. Deeper berries, reds and bold tones make confident statement looks for evenings or special occasions.
